About the data

This page replays the counts of 1,070 Scottish council elections from 2007, 2012, 2017 and 2022, each decided by single transferable vote (STV). Pick a ward on the left to watch its count.

Where it comes from

The data and this page's code are in the mggg/scottish-ballot-clustering-replication repository on GitHub. David McCune assembled and cleaned the ballot records from Scottish sources. They are in data/scot-elex/, one file per election with every ballot's ranking and each candidate's party. The page counts each election from those ballots itself. The published round-by-round results in data/scottish_vote_tables/ serve as a check: the page's counts match all 1,070 of them exactly.

How a count works

A candidate wins on reaching the quota: the total votes divided by one more than the number of seats, rounded down, plus one. Each round moves one candidate's ballots on to the next choice still in the race. A winner's surplus above the quota goes first, earliest winner first: every ballot the winner holds moves on at a fraction of its value, rounded down to five decimal places, so that together they carry just the surplus. With no surplus left to move, the candidate in last place is eliminated and all their ballots move on. Ballots that rank no one still in the race, and the fractions lost to rounding, are non-transferable.

What the colours mean

Bars use party colours: Labour, Conservative, Liberal Democrat, Green and SNP have their own, and every other party, independents included, shares the independents' teal. Candidates sharing a colour get lighter shades in ballot order, which is alphabetical by surname. The page follows every ballot through the count, so each chunk of a bar shows which candidate those voters ranked first, even after their votes have passed through other candidates. When votes move, they fly from the candidate passing them on, coloured by the same first choices. A winner's bar turns grey, and the strip along its bottom shows their voters' first choices. Non-transferable votes collect in the bottom row, hatched in the colour of the voters' first choice. Hover over or tap a row for a table of its votes by first choice.

Controls

Replay restarts the count. Pause stops between steps, and Previous step and Next step move one step at a time; click any step in the list to jump to it. Speed and Order change how fast the count plays and how candidates are sorted. Download MP4 replays the count and saves it as a video.
