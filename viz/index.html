About the data

This page replays the counts of 1,070 Scottish council elections from 2007, 2012, 2017 and 2022, each decided by single transferable vote (STV). Pick a ward on the left to watch its count.

Where it comes from

The data and this page's code are in the mggg/scottish-ballot-clustering-replication repository on GitHub. David McCune assembled and cleaned the ballot records from Scottish sources. They are in data/scot-elex/, one file per election with every ballot's ranking and each candidate's party. The counts come from the published round-by-round results in data/scottish_vote_tables/, which list every candidate's total after each round.

How a count works

A candidate wins on reaching the quota: the total votes divided by one more than the number of seats, rounded down, plus one. Each round moves one candidate's votes on to the next choice on each ballot: either a winner's surplus above the quota, or every vote of the candidate in last place. Votes whose ballots rank no one still in the race are non-transferable. The vote tables only record each round's totals, so the page reads each transfer from whose totals rise, and counts the shortfall as non-transferable.

What the colors mean

Bars use party colors: Labour, Conservative, Liberal Democrat, Green and SNP have their own, and every other party, independents included, shares the independents' teal. Candidates sharing a color get lighter shades in ballot order, which is alphabetical by surname. Each chunk of a bar shows the candidate who last passed those votes on. Because the vote tables only record totals, they can't show who the voters first chose: a vote can pass through several candidates before it settles.

When a candidate is elected, their bar squashes down into a strip along its bottom, and the rest turns gray. When their surplus moves on, the gray bar and its strip squash back to the quota, keeping the same mix of colors, and the surplus above the quota, now in the winner's own color, flies to the candidates it transfers to. Non-transferable votes collect in the bottom row, hatched in the color of the candidate whose transfer lost them. Hover over or tap a row for a table of who last passed its votes on.

Controls

Replay restarts the count. Pause stops between steps, and Previous step and Next step move one step at a time; click any step in the list to jump to it. Speed and Order change how fast the count plays and how candidates are sorted. Download MP4 replays the count and saves it as a video.
