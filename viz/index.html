About the data

This page replays the counts of 1,070 Scottish council elections from 2007, 2012, 2017 and 2022, each decided by single transferable vote (STV). Pick a ward on the left to watch its count.

Where it comes from

The data and this page's code are in the mggg/scottish-ballot-clustering-replication repository on GitHub. David McCune assembled and cleaned the ballot records from Scottish sources. They are in data/scot-elex/, one file per election with every ballot and each candidate's party. Each vote table in data/scottish_vote_tables/ lists every candidate's total after each round of the count.

How a count works

A candidate wins on reaching the quota: the total votes divided by one more than the number of seats, rounded down, plus one. Each round moves one candidate's votes on to the next choice on each ballot: either a winner's surplus above the quota, or every vote of the candidate in last place. Votes whose ballots rank no one still in the race are non-transferable. The vote tables only record totals, so the page reads each transfer from whose totals rise, and counts the shortfall as non-transferable.

What the colours mean

Bars use party colours, lighter for each later candidate of a party in alphabetical order. Each chunk of a bar shows the candidate who last passed those votes on, not who the voters first chose: a vote can pass through several candidates before it settles. A winner's bar turns grey, and the strip along its bottom shows the voters' first choices instead. The page works those out by replaying the ballots in the order the vote table records, which reproduces every table to within 0.05 votes. Non-transferable votes collect in the bottom row, hatched in the colour of the candidate whose transfer lost them. Hover over or tap a row for a table of who last passed its votes on.

Controls

Pause stops between steps, and Previous step and Next step move one step at a time. Click any step in the list to jump to it. Download replays the count and saves it as a video.
